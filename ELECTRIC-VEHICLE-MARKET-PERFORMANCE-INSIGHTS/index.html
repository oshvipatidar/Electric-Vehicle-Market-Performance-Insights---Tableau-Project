What the analysis covers

Eight analytical perspectives

The dashboard contains eight analytical worksheets that explore the dataset from manufacturer, technology, geography, time, range and customer perspectives.

01 / MANUFACTURERS

Manufacturer sales performance

Compare manufacturers using the annual-sales measure included in the analysis.

02 / TECHNOLOGY

Battery technology distribution

Explore how the vehicle records are distributed across battery technologies.

03 / GEOGRAPHY

Manufacturing country analysis

Review the countries represented in the electric-vehicle dataset.

04 / TIME

EV sales by model year

Explore the annual pattern across model years represented in the data.

05 / RANGE

Driving range comparison

Compare average driving-range performance across manufacturers.

06 / BATTERY

Battery capacity and driving range

Investigate the relationship between battery capacity and vehicle range.

07 / CUSTOMER

Customer satisfaction

Explore the customer-rating measure across manufacturers.

08 / BATTERY

Battery capacity by technology

Compare average battery capacity across battery technology types.
